## 1. How to Add a New Category

Categories help parents find baby clothes in your store menu (such as **SHOP**, **GIRLS**, **BOYS**, or **NEWBORN**).

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. In the left navigation menu, click **Categories & Menu** (under Website Management).
2. Click the brown button at the top right: **+ Add Main Category**.
3. Follow the guided 5-step process:
   - **STEP 1 — Category Details:**
     - Type the **Category Name** (e.g. `Party Dresses` or `Baby Essentials`).
     - Type the **Page Title** (the heading parents see on top of the shopping page).
     - Set the **Display Order** (e.g. `1` for first, `2` for second).
     - Click **Save & Continue →**.
   - **STEP 2 — Add Images:**
     - Click **Upload Banner** to choose a high-quality picture from your computer.
     - Click **Save & Continue →**.
   - **STEP 3 — Choose Products:**
     - Check the boxes next to the baby outfits that should appear on this page.
     - Click **Save & Continue →**.
   - **STEP 4 — Description:**
     - Write 1 or 2 friendly sentences explaining the clothes in this category.
     - Click **Save & Continue →**.
   - **STEP 5 — Check & SEO:**
     - Review the page details.
4. Click **Publish Page** to make it live, or click **Save Draft** if you want to work on it later.

## 2. How to Add Submenu Items

Submenu items appear inside dropdown menus (for example, clicking or hovering over **GIRLS** opens **Party Dresses**, **Cashmere Cardigans**, etc.).

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Go to **Categories & Menu**.
2. Find the main category where you want the new item to go (for example, **GIRLS** or **BOYS**).
3. Click the small **+ Add Submenu Item** button under that category.
4. Enter the details:
   - **Submenu Name:** e.g. `Party Dresses`.
   - **Parent Category:** Choose which main menu it belongs under.
   - **Short Description:** e.g. `Delicate twirl-ready party dresses for celebrations.`
5. Choose which products belong in this subcategory.
6. Click **Save & Continue** and then **Publish Page**.
7. The submenu item will now appear in your website header automatically without modifying any code.

## 3. How to Upload Images

You can upload pictures directly from your computer for product cards, category banners, and website pages.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. In the left navigation menu, click **Media Library** (or use the upload button inside the product/category form).
2. Click **Upload Images** or drag and drop image files from your computer into the upload box.
3. Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP, and SVG files up to 8MB.
4. Once uploaded:
   - The picture is safely saved in the store.
   - Click **Copy Link** to copy the picture's web address whenever you want to paste it into another page.
   - To remove a photo, click the trash can icon.

## 4. How to Add a Baby Product

Add rompers, dresses, sleepsuits, knitwear, and nursery items to your boutique catalog.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. In the left menu, click **Products** (under Store Management).
2. Click the brown button: **+ Add Baby Outfit**.
3. Follow the 4-step wizard:
   - **Step 1: Basic Info**
     - Type the **Item Name** (e.g. `Aurelia Organic Cashmere Romper`).
     - Choose the **Category** (e.g. `Baby Clothing` or `Dresses`).
     - Enter the **Product Description** (explain fabric softness, organic certification, and comfort).
     - Click **Continue →**.
   - **Step 2: Photos**
     - Click **Choose Images** to pick photos from your computer.
     - You can upload multiple photos. Click **Set as Main** on the photo you want parents to see first.
     - Click **Continue →**.
   - **Step 3: Pricing & Stock**
     - Enter the **Selling Price** (e.g. `18500`).
     - Enter the **Original Price** if on sale (leave blank or same if not on sale).
     - Enter the **Total Stock Quantity** (e.g. `25`).
     - Click **Continue →**.
   - **Step 4: Sizes & Badges**
     - Select available sizes: click on `Newborn (0–3M)`, `3–6 Months`, `6–12 Months`, etc.
     - Select colours: click on `Ivory Cream`, `Blush Pink`, etc.
     - Check labels if needed: `★ Best Seller` or `✨ New Arrival`.
4. Click **Preview Outfit** to see how the outfit looks.
5. Click **Publish Outfit** to make it available for purchase immediately.

## 5. How to Set and Update Prices

Manage selling prices, discounts, and sale tags.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Go to **Promotions & Sale** (under Promotions) or **Products**.
2. Find the product you want to change.
3. Click **Edit Price**:
   - **Selling Price:** The price parents pay at checkout.
   - **Original Price:** If you enter an original price higher than the selling price, the store will automatically display a **Sale Badge** and calculate the discount percentage (e.g. `20% OFF`).
4. Click **Save Prices**. The price updates on the website immediately.

## 6. How to Assign Products to Categories

Choose which baby outfits appear when a parent opens a category or collection.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Go to **Categories & Menu** (or **Collections**).
2. Click **Edit** on the category or collection you wish to update.
3. Open the **STEP 3 — Choose Products** tab.
4. You will see a list of all your baby clothes with photos and prices:
   - Check the box next to any outfit you want to include.
   - Uncheck the box if you want to remove an outfit from that page.
   - Use the search bar to find products quickly.
5. Click **Save & Continue** and then **Publish Page**.

---

## 7. How to Preview a Page Before Customers See It

Check how your changes will look before making them live.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. While editing any category, website page, or product, look at the bottom of the form for the **Preview** button.
2. Click **Preview Page** or **Preview Outfit**:
   - A live window opens showing the real design, photos, title, and buttons.
   - Use the **Desktop** and **Mobile** buttons at the top to see how it looks on computers vs. mobile phones.
3. When finished checking, click **✕ Close Preview**.
4. None of your draft changes are published until you explicitly click **Publish**.

## 8. How to Publish a Page or Category

Move your work from private draft mode to live on the website.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. In the category or page editor, make sure required fields (like Title/Name) are filled out.
2. Click the brown button: **Publish Page** (or **Publish Outfit**).
3. The system will save your changes and display a success message: `"Category is now published! 🌸"`.
4. Parents will now be able to see the page when clicking your website menu.

## 9. How to Update Existing Information

Change titles, descriptions, banner photos, or contact info anytime.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. Go to the relevant admin section:
   - For menu titles or banners: **Categories & Menu**.
   - For clothes, prices, or photos: **Products**.
   - For About, Shipping, or FAQ: **Website Pages**.
   - For store phone, email, or address: **Store Settings**.
   - For Google search preview: **SEO Settings**.
2. Click **Edit** on the item you want to change.
3. Make your text or photo adjustments.
4. Click **Save Changes** or **Publish**.
5. Your updates appear on the live storefront immediately.

## 10. How to Hide or Safely Remove Content

Hide seasonal items or remove old content without losing data.

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
- **To Hide a Category or Submenu Temporarily:**
  1. Go to **Categories & Menu**.
  2. Find the category.
  3. Click **Hide from Website** (eye icon).
  4. The category will become a private draft and disappear from the website menu, but all its settings and products remain saved for you to turn back on later.
- **To Delete an Item Permanently:**
  1. Click the trash can icon (**Delete**) next to the category, product, or collection.
  2. A confirmation prompt will ask: `"Are you sure you want to delete this item?"`.
  3. Click **Cancel** if you changed your mind, or **Yes, Delete** to confirm.
  4. The item is safely removed from the catalog.
